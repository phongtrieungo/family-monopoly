- (capability) CAP-1: Families set up 2–4 seats on one device with explicit handoffs and recover the local game.
- (capability) CAP-2: Families join private rooms across devices and play the authored full match with independently authenticated seats.
- (capability) CAP-3: Players follow server-accepted turns, auctions, cards, trades, debt and winner with accessible controls.
- (capability) CAP-4: Authorized players recover their committed match and pending decisions after refresh, lost responses and server restart.
- (capability) CAP-5: A player reclaims only their seat on a new device with a private recovery code; the old session is invalidated.
- (constraint) Server authority, deterministic replay, pinned content, revision guards, idempotent receipts, authorization and redacted projections bind all capabilities.
- (constraint) Preserve existing rule, policy, scenario and requirement IDs; apply only approved rule changes and use cleared original content.
- (constraint) The remote pilot targets at most four people and accepts brief maintenance pauses. Local mode shares a trusted device; private mode never shares seat sessions.
- (decision) If both the browser session and private recovery code are lost, the family starts a new match; neither invite nor host can reset the seat.
- (question) How are recovery codes issued, protected, rotated and recovered safely after a lost response?
- (question) What hosting, durable storage, backup, retention, abandonment and operational policies will be approved for the remote pilot?
- (question) Who will review family usability, Vietnamese, accessibility, devices, branding and distribution before wider play?
- (direction) Adopt architecture spine AD-1–AD-6 and existing linked requirements as companions. Existing ADR-003 and EPIC-003 defer device recovery; new owner decision sets the future requirement without claiming implementation.
- (event) Coherence pass: five stable unique capabilities each carry intent and testable success; constraints, non-goals and open questions are explicit. PASS.
- (event) Preservation pass: game, existing room/local/full-match contracts, scenarios, epic proposals, B-09 gates and architecture spine remain adopted companions; owner recovery decision takes precedence for future design while older deferrals are explicitly identified for reconciliation.
