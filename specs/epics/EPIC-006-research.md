# EPIC-006 · Digital board-game references

Reviewed 2026-11-14 using publisher materials and official design guidance. These references inform the interaction and art direction. The findings reflect published materials, and usability in our game still needs to be checked with the ST-601 fixtures. No third-party assets were imported.

| Reference | Published evidence | Application to EPIC-006 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ticket to Ride · Marmalade Game Studio | The product page presents the maps as the setting for building lines, with a gallery of digital board screenshots. Product page undated. [Official game page and screenshot gallery](https://www.marmaladegamestudio.com/games/ticket-to-ride) | Give the shared board the largest continuous area. Make locations and routes visually distinct. Put space details behind a selection on the board or its readable index. ST-601–602. |
| New MONOPOLY · Ubisoft | The product page describes buying, selling and trading within an animated city. Game release 2024-09-26. [Official product page](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/games/new-monopoly) | Tie feedback to the affected token or property. Translate the neighborhood idea into original flat Vietnamese illustrations and retain a stable top-down view during movement. ST-601–603. |
| Wingspan · Monster Couch | The developer's 2026-09-07 patch adds an on-demand card-tray preview with cards and counters, available beside the overview. [Official patch notes](https://www.monstercouch.com/wingspan-patch-september-7/) | Keep secondary state one action away. Use inspection for the full deed or portfolio and a history panel for earlier events. Keep essential prices, obligations and outcomes in the current turn view. ST-602, ST-608, ST-615. |
| Board Game Arena · Studio UX guidelines | Sections A.1–A.5 cover centered boards, a concise current-action bar, compact player panels and layouts that work at default zoom. Publication date unlisted. [Official Studio guidelines](https://doc.studio.boardgamearena.com/assets/framework-documentation/guidelines/Guidelines_UX.pdf) | Use one decision area that identifies who acts, what they can do and what it costs. Use compact seat summaries, allow board pieces to open their detail, and give optional detail a predictable route back to the table. ST-601, ST-602 and the [decision migration](EPIC-006-decisions.md). |

The visual review should test whether a family can find the active token, name the person who needs to act, state the financial consequence of their choice, and explain the previous outcome from the table or its details. Record the observed results with the reviewed fixtures in ST-601.
